Club Rules

Keep it safe, productive, and fun.

01 Respect Everyone

Be kind. No bullying, harassment, hate speech, or putting others down. If someone asks you to stop, stop immediately.

02 Keep It Clean

No swearing or inappropriate jokes, comments, images, or topics. This is a 13+ space.

03 Stay Focused

Don’t distract people who are working. Stay on topic. If you’re bored or done early, tell a leader or helper.

04 Respect Privacy

Don’t share personal info. Don’t take or post photos/videos without permission. No threatening, blackmailing, or sharing private messages.

05 Listen to Staff

Don’t interrupt. Follow instructions from leaders and moderators. Their decisions are final.

06 Respect Equipment

Take care of your device and others’ property. Don’t touch someone else’s laptop without asking.

07 Clean Up

Help pack up, throw away trash, and leave the space clean.

Failure to follow rules may result in removal from club.
