What we do

We are a proud chapter of Hack Club, a global non-profit community of high schoolers building the future. At Hack Club Curaçao, we believe the best way to learn is by actually making things; not just watching tutorials.

Weekly Meetings

Think of this as a "gym for programmers." We hang out, share snacks, and work on whatever we want. Whether you're building a website, a game, or a robot, there’s always someone nearby to help when you get stuck.

Hackathons

A "hackathon" isn't about breaking into banks; it's an invention marathon. We get together for 12–24 hours to build a project from scratch. It's high-energy, full of collaboration, and the fastest way to level up your skills.

YSWS (You Ship, We Ship)

This is the magic of the Hack Club network. When you finish a project ("ship it"), you can get real-world rewards. We’ve had members get free $100 electronics grants, professional software stickers, and even custom-made circuit boards just for showing what they built.

International Impact

Hack Club is a global 501(c)(3) nonprofit supported by visionary partners like Elon Musk, Tom Preston-Werner (GitHub), and OpenAI.

Our network of 400+ clubs in 50+ countries has empowered over 30,000 student hackers to build projects that make a real impact. When you join Hack Club Curaçao, you're not just joining a local club — you're becoming part of a worldwide movement of young creators shaping the future.
